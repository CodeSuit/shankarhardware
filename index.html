ABOUT SHANKAR HARDWARE

Built on Durability, Honesty & Farmer Trust

Established in the heart of Maharashtra's agricultural community in Sonwadi (Pune District), Shankar Hardware & Agro Tech has grown from a local spare parts store to a comprehensive destination for heavy-duty farming equipment and civil construction supplies.

We understand that during peak sowing, irrigation, or harvesting seasons, machine downtime costs farmers dearly. That's why our shelves in Sonwadi are perpetually stocked with genuine impellers, submersible pumps, non-corrosive valves, and high-tensile hardware that withstand tough conditions.
